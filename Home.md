## Workflow

1. Capture rough notes in `00 Inbox`.
2. Move them into University, Work, Personal, Projects, or Reference when they become useful.
3. Put reusable note templates in `90 Templates`.
4. Attachments are stored automatically in `80 Attachments`.
5. Move inactive material to `99 Archive` rather than deleting it when the history may still be useful.
